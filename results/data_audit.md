## Interpretation

The experiment removes exact duplicate *rows* before the stratified train/test split to prevent identical records appearing in both partitions. Identical questionnaire answers may, however, come from different people; without patient identifiers this cannot be checked. Therefore deduplication is a modeling choice, not a verified patient-level cleaning step.

This single-site, symptom-questionnaire dataset is insufficient for clinical deployment or population-level generalization claims.
